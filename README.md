## Featured Projects

### KodiSalama (v1.3.0) — LIVE
> Mfumo wa kisasa na **salama** wa usimamizi wa kodi za nyumba kwa wamiliki wa nyumba nchini Tanzania. Sasa ni **multi-tenant SaaS** yenye akaunti za wamiliki wengi, kila mmoja ana data zake zilizojitenga kabisa.

**Vipengele muhimu:**
- 👤 **Multi-tenant** — wamiliki wengi, data zimejitenga kwa ownerId kwenye kila jedwali
- 💬 **SMS Gateway** — tuma risiti na vikumbusho moja kwa moja kupitia Africa's Talking (sandbox + live)
- 📜 **Sera ya Faragha na Masharti** — watumiaji lazima wakubali kabla ya kujiunga (timestamped)
- 🏢 **Vyumba na Wapangaji** — fuatilia occupancy, malipo, madeni
- 📄 **Mikataba** — tengeneza mikataba rasmi yenye bendera ya Tanzania
- 💰 **Malipo** — rekodi malipo ya cash, M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel, benki
- 📊 **Dashibodi** — ripoti za mapato, collection rate, top debtors

[🌐 Live App](https://kodisalama.vercel.app) · [📦 Source Code](https://github.com/galaxymushizo-netizen/kodisalama) · [📋 Releases](https://github.com/galaxymushizo-netizen/kodisalama/releases)
